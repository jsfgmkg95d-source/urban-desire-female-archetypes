# Urban Desire Female Archetypes

Use this repository to create, audit, index, or retrieve reusable female-character mechanisms for modern urban fiction.

Before changing assets, read `AGENTS.md` and follow its evidence, age, visual-analysis, originality, batching, and repository-isolation rules.

Keep `Fact`, `Interpretation`, and `Adaptation` visibly separate. Record unconfirmed source claims as `UNKNOWN`; do not fill gaps by inference. Preserve source and version metadata so later audits can trace each factual claim.

Store character assets under the appropriate `characters/` category, machine-readable derivatives under `data/`, retrieval aids under `indexes/`, source evidence under `sources/`, and batch audit records under `batches/`. Use `templates/` and `references/` when they exist and are relevant.

Extract mechanisms that can drive recognition, contrast, desire, relationships, misunderstanding, leverage, danger, and plot. Do not reproduce a classic character's original plot as an original-fiction storyline.

Do not begin large-scale generation without an explicitly approved, small validated batch and completed fact and duplication audits.
